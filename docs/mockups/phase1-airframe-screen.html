Rules the implementation honours

Retro instrument shapes

Compact rounded buttons and fields use crisp borders, restrained radii and no shadow. Controls remain text-only.

Fields show their role

Editable values sit in compact wells; calculated values are unboxed and blue. This distinction remains visible as parameterisation adapts automatically.

Cobalt means interaction

Selection, calculated values, focus, and the primary action. Warning and valid design states retain separate semantic colours.

Nothing floats over the drawing

Except the selection card, which is anchored to the selected part by a leader line. Chips and readout live in the footer bar.

Inputs adapt automatically

The app infers a compatible driver set from what you enter, calculates the remaining values, and reports under- or over-constrained geometry without a mode selector.

The drawing never scrolls

It fills its space at all times. Card content may scroll internally when a component has many fields.
